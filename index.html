How it works

40,000 Blocks • … per Block

  1. Pick your blocks. Click any open square on the grid above. Select as few or as many as your brand needs — build a logo shape, a strip, or a single square.
  2. Check out. Add your brand name, website link, and logo, then pay securely by card.
  3. Go live. Once payment clears, your logo appears on the wall automatically, linking straight to your site — permanently.

Blocks are sold individually and are yours once payment is confirmed. Reserved (in-checkout) blocks are held for 15 minutes before releasing back to the pool if payment isn't completed.
